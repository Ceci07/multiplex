# In silico assessment of primers targeting different AMR gene families
An R script compared ABRicate detections from in silico predicted amplicons with whole-genome sequencing (WGS), used as the reference, at the sample-by-panel-target level. Mapping the theoretical panel targets and calculating overall and target-specific sensitivity and precision, identifying recovered and missed targets, and reporting genes detected by WGS that fall outside the panel.
